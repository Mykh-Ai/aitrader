# SHI_RESET_40F — direction ablation, all expiry cases and stop interventions

Date: 2026-10-14. User scope: assess the weak short contribution, inspect all six seven-day closures, identify what unites the 16 stops and test how to reduce losing outcomes. Run root: `market_monitor_runs/SHI_RESET_40F_direction_expiry_stop_interventions_v0/`. Frozen baseline: 40D directional hybrid; prior diagnosis: 40E. This is isolated offline research, not production policy or an active legacy Backtester gate.

## Frozen scope and clocks

`study_manifest.json` declares 16 variants and both intervention script SHA256 hashes before returns. They include baseline, long-only, short-only, separate/both-direction opposing-H4 space screens, one additional long confirmation H1, early reference invalidation, short profit-protection and three shorter deadlines. Baseline targets/stops/costs remain fixed except the specifically declared intervention. All variants use the full original feed chronology, global one-position book, three-minute cooldown, $3,000 notional, $5 round-trip fee, 2-bps adverse slippage per fill and strict censoring for extended/recovered data. Isolated-minute skips retain actual UTC time.

The bounded H4 inventory carries confirmed prior references chronologically, with strict three-bar swing confirmation at the right H4 close, two usable H1 closes beyond a 5-bp band for retirement, and 30-day maximum age. This is a research proxy, not a promoted canonical Monitor registry. Space is measured at the decision from the known H1 close and then-known initial-stop estimate. Future entry open is not a filter input. No known opposing level means the screen passes, not that all possible structure is absent.

One-hour long confirmation creates a new decision after the extra completed usable H1 and reapplies quality/calendar restrictions. It never backdates entry to the original signal. Early reference failure is known only after a subsequent H1 close and executes at the next real open no earlier than close plus one minute. Protective orders remain effective; a scheduled market open does not consume later intraminute high/low. Profit protection is triggered by a completed minute reaching 1R and moves the stop to +0.25R after at least one-minute latency; it never captures the trigger minute's earlier price movement. Borrow interest and actual BTCUSDC exchange fills remain unmodeled.

## All stops and actual interventions

All 16 stops stay below 2R, and 13 below 1R. This is an outcome observation, not a hindsight entry filter. Twelve face a known opposing H4 reference within 1R versus five of ten non-stop outcomes; the feature has winning counterexamples. Six/eight long stops lose the frozen entry EMA within the first three H1 closes. The original code has price/EMA or two-close acceptance, volume/delta tests and fixed protective exits, but lacks an opposing-structure-space admission check and retest-holding evidence after the trigger. These are contract gaps and supported hypotheses, not proof of a single market cause for every stop.

Best completely observed dollar result: `SHORT_SPACE_1R`. It blocks short if the next known opposite H4 band is less than one estimated initial risk from the decision close. Full history: 21 observed trades, +801.281956 USD gross, -105 fees, -25.231848 slippage = **+671.050107 USD**. Six-month April–September entry cohort: 19 trades, **+839.785861 USD**. Full mean +31.954767 USD /1.119495R, mean initial risk48.144574 USD; net R sum23.509400. Long15 trades/+387.165263; short6/+283.884844. Closed-trade drawdown224.013800 USD, PF2.004404. Three isolated omissions, zero unresolved or inferred-gap marks.

Compared with baseline: negative trades17 ->13; stop exits16 ->12; positive trades9 ->8; positive fraction34.615% ->38.095%; net512.276015 ->671.050107; closed-trade drawdown459.172245 ->224.013800. **Absolute positive count did not increase.** Six original short losses totaling390.262072 USD are avoided, one original positive short58.774416 is lost, and two new losses total172.713563. Net improvement158.774093 USD. This is a fresh chronological portfolio, not selective removal of losing rows.

`BOTH_SPACE_1R` offers a different tradeoff: 10 trades, four positive/six negative, five stop exits, +614.468016 USD, closed-trade drawdown240.351632. It removes more losing trades but also more winners and total opportunities. All variants and rejections are retained.

Counterevidence: short protection at +0.25R after1R increases positive trades to13 but reduces model net to373.482955 USD, below the user monetary benchmark. Early short reclaim exits also remove winners. Long early invalidation/additional-H1 confirmation and all three shortened deadlines produce extended-gap censored portfolios; they cannot be described as complete positive results. Seven of the16 variants contain one censored trade each; reported sums there are resolved cash only. All incomplete/negative variants remain in `intervention_results.csv` and `variant_ledgers/`.
