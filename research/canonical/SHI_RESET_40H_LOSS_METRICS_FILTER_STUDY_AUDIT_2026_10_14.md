All new raw windows use actualUTC `(decision-hours,decision]`, containing no future entry observation. Delta percent is100×(sumBuy−sumSell)/sumVolume; not the mean of minute delta percentages. Monitor percent rounding is reconciled with raw values.4h means a rolling window, not a calendarH4 candle. Windows tolerate an isolated missing/synthetic minute, preserving timestamps and omission evidence; recovered/extended gaps remain separate. Funding/liquidations are signal evidence, not spot financing charges. Clock filters use the planned entry `decision+1min`; all accepted entries occupy the same hour as that known planned entry. Budapest uses `Europe/Budapest` with DST, not a fixed offset.
